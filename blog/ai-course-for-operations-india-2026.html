AI in Operations — What Operations Managers Actually Need to Know

Operations managers in India are now expected to understand and implement AI in their functions: demand forecasting with ML, predictive maintenance on equipment, automated quality control using computer vision, AI-powered route optimisation for logistics, and supply chain risk sensing. Managers who can bridge the gap between operations expertise and AI capability are leading these transformations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which AI tool is most important for supply chain in India 2026?

Demand forecasting ML (integrated in SAP IBP, Blue Yonder, Kinaxis) is most widely deployed. AI route optimisation (LogiNext, FarEye for logistics). Predictive maintenance (ThyssenKrupp, Siemens MindSphere for manufacturing). For managers, understanding the business application of these tools matters more than technical implementation.

Can operations managers learn AI without an engineering background?

Yes — the most relevant AI skills for operations managers (demand forecasting interpretation, analytics dashboards, supply chain risk sensing) are business-oriented, not engineering-oriented. IIM analytics programmes are specifically designed for this profile — no coding or engineering background needed.

How does AI help in manufacturing in India 2026?

AI applications in Indian manufacturing: predictive maintenance (reducing downtime in automotive, pharma, FMCG), quality control via computer vision (defect detection at speeds humans cannot match), demand-driven production planning (ML forecasting reducing overproduction), and energy optimisation (AI-controlled HVAC and equipment scheduling reducing energy costs 15-30%).
