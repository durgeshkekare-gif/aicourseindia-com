AI has entered every major HR function in 2026. Recruitment uses AI for resume screening, video interview analysis and candidate matching. Retention uses ML models to predict attrition 90 days before it happens. L&D uses GenAI to personalise learning paths. Compensation benchmarking uses real-time market data through AI dashboards. HR professionals who cannot engage with these tools are losing credibility in strategic discussions.

People Analytics — The Core Skill

People analytics is the most immediately applicable AI skill for HR professionals. It involves using data to answer HR questions: Which employees are likely to leave in the next 6 months? What compensation structures reduce attrition? Which recruitment channels produce the highest-performing employees? Tools range from Excel-based to Python-based to vendor platforms (Visier, Workday Analytics).

GenAI for HR — What Actually Changes

  • JD writing: GenAI drafts 80% of a JD in 30 seconds — HRBP refines and approves
  • Interview question banks: GenAI generates competency-based interview questions from JD
  • Policy documents: GenAI drafts policies — legal/HR refines for compliance
  • L&D content: GenAI creates course outlines, quizzes and case studies at 10x speed
  • Employee communication: GenAI drafts engagement surveys, feedback forms, and pulse check tools

Frequently Asked Questions

Can HR professionals do AI course without coding?

Yes — IIM Kozhikode Analytics & GenAI, Wharton People Analytics on Coursera, and SHRM AI in HRM are all designed for HR professionals without coding backgrounds. These teach analytics interpretation, GenAI tools, and data-driven HR decisions — not programming.

What is people analytics?

People analytics (also called HR analytics or workforce analytics) is the use of data and statistical methods to make better HR decisions. Common applications: attrition prediction, high-performer identification, compensation benchmarking, recruitment funnel optimisation, and L&D impact measurement.

What GenAI tools are HR professionals using in India 2026?

Most commonly used GenAI tools in Indian HR 2026: ChatGPT/Claude for JD writing and policy drafting, Microsoft Copilot in Teams for meeting summaries, Workday Illuminate for people insights, Darwinbox AI for Indian HR platforms, and Excel Copilot for workforce planning. IIM GenAI courses cover the strategic use of these tools.
